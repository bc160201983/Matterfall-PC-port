**Matterfall** (PS4, CUSA02475) PC port by **BilalReloaded**
([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@BilalReloaded/videos), [GitHub](https://github.com/bc160201983)).

Download **Matterfall-windows.zip** below and extract it over your folder (settings and saves are
kept). No game files are included.

## Fixed

- **Voice lines too loud** ("multiplier up", "multiplier lost", "health low"; issue #2). On PS4
  these play on the controller's own small speaker; the port played them on your speakers at full
  volume. They now play at 35%, and the launcher has a setting for it: **Audio -> Controller
  speaker sounds** (Off to 100%).

## Crashes while loading (issues #1 and #2)

The "Ran out of memory" crash and the "Serial size mismatch" crash after dying both came from the
game reading wrong data from its files, fixed in **1.0.2**. If you saw them on 1.0.0 or 1.0.1,
please update. If one still happens on 1.0.3, open an issue with the log (launcher -> Advanced ->
Open log folder, `launcher-last-run.log`).

## Known issues

- Ultra: lit surfaces sometimes render black. Use High or restart the game if you see it.
- The game checks its save about every 15 seconds, which can show as a short hitch.
